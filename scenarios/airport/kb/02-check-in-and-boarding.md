# Check-in, gates and boarding

SOURCE: https://www.solsticeair.example/help/check-in

## Timing
- Online check-in opens 30 hours before departure and closes 75 minutes before.
- Bag drop closes 45 minutes before departure on short-haul routes and 60
  minutes before on long-haul.
- Gates close 20 minutes before departure. A passenger who has not boarded by
  then is offloaded along with their bags.

## Boarding pass
- A digital pass on a phone is accepted throughout Kestrel International.
- Keep screen brightness up; the gate scanners struggle with dimmed displays.
- A passenger travelling with a checked bag still needs to visit bag drop even
  when already checked in.

## DO NOT
- Do not quote a cutoff for a specific flight from these notes. Cutoffs shift
  with aircraft type and stand allocation; use a live lookup.
