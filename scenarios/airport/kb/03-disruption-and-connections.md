# Delays, cancellations and missed connections

SOURCE: https://www.solsticeair.example/help/disruption

## When a flight is delayed
- Passengers are rebooked automatically on the next available Solstice service;
  no queueing is required.
- Refreshment vouchers are issued once a delay passes two hours, and a hotel is
  arranged once an overnight stay becomes unavoidable.
- A passenger who leaves the airport during a delay must tell staff first, or
  the automatic rebooking is cancelled.

## Missed connections
- A connection missed because of a Solstice delay is rebooked at no cost, and
  the onward seat is held while that happens.
- Checked bags are transferred automatically and follow the passenger to the new
  flight; they do not need to be reclaimed and re-checked.
- Minimum connection time at Kestrel International is 45 minutes within the same
  terminal and 75 minutes between terminals.

## DO NOT
- Do not promise compensation, an amount, or an entitlement under any specific
  regulation. Those depend on route, cause and jurisdiction; route them to a
  human or a live lookup.
