# Cabin baggage and restricted items

SOURCE: https://www.solsticeair.example/help/cabin-baggage

## Allowance
- One cabin bag plus one personal item (a handbag, a small backpack, or a laptop
  sleeve) per passenger.
- The cabin bag must fit the gate sizer. Anything larger is tagged at the gate
  and travels in the hold at no charge.
- Weight limits vary by fare and by departure airport. Quote the general rule
  only; route any specific allowance question to a booking lookup.

## Power banks and lithium batteries
- Power banks travel in the cabin only, never in checked baggage.
- Spare batteries must have their terminals taped or sit in their original
  retail packaging.
- Devices with a non-removable battery may be checked if they are switched fully
  off, not merely asleep.

## Liquids
- Containers of 100 ml or less, together in one transparent resealable bag of
  about a litre.
- Baby food, prescription medicine and duty-free purchases sealed in a tamper
  evident bag are outside this limit.

## DO NOT
- Do not state a fee, a weight in kilograms, or an airport-specific rule from
  these notes. Those change without warning and differ by route; route them to a
  tool instead.
