# What counts as an emergency, and what comes before us

SOURCE: https://www.ridgelinehvac.example/help/emergencies

## Not our call first
- A gas smell, a carbon monoxide alarm, visible sparking or a burning smell is a
  safety matter before it is a repair.
- The caller leaves the building, touches no switch or appliance on the way out,
  and calls their gas utility's emergency line from outside.
- We attend afterwards. We never ask a caller to investigate, isolate or test
  anything themselves.

## Priority dispatch
Treated as priority and pushed to the front of the queue:
- No heat when it is at or below freezing.
- No cooling during a heat advisory.
- Water leaking from a unit onto floors or into ceilings.
- Any of the above where an infant, an elderly person, or someone with a medical
  need is in the home.

## Standard booking
Everything else: intermittent noises, weak airflow, a thermostat behaving oddly,
routine servicing, a unit that is working but not well.

## DO NOT
- Do not talk a caller through anything involving gas, electrical panels or
  refrigerant.
- Do not promise an arrival time. Give the window the dispatch system returns.
