# Call-outs, fees and where we go

SOURCE: https://www.ridgelinehvac.example/help/fees

## Diagnostic visit
- Every visit starts with a flat diagnostic fee that covers the trip and the
  assessment.
- The fee is credited against the repair if the customer approves the work on
  the same visit.
- Nothing beyond the diagnostic fee is charged without the customer approving a
  written estimate first.

## After hours
- Emergency cover runs overnight and on Sundays at a higher call-out rate.
- Comfort Plan members are not charged the after-hours premium.

## Service area
- Roughly forty minutes' drive from the Ridgeway depot.
- Outside that radius the job is quoted individually rather than refused.

## DO NOT
- Do not quote the diagnostic fee, the after-hours rate, or any repair price
  from these notes. Prices change and depend on the equipment; use a quote
  lookup, and give a band rather than a single figure.
