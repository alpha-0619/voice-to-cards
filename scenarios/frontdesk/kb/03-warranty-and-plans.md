# Warranty and the Comfort Plan

SOURCE: https://www.ridgelinehvac.example/help/warranty

## Manufacturer warranty
- Equipment we supplied and installed carries the manufacturer's parts warranty,
  registered by us at installation.
- Labour is covered separately and for a shorter period than parts, so a repair
  can be partly covered.
- Warranty does not cover damage from a failed filter change, blocked returns or
  work done by someone else.

## Comfort Plan
- Two scheduled services a year, spring and autumn.
- Priority scheduling ahead of non-members during a cold snap or a heatwave.
- No after-hours premium.
- A standing discount on parts.

## Filters
- Customers change their own filters; it is the one maintenance task we ask them
  to do, and skipping it causes a large share of the failures we attend.
- A filter is not a warranty visit.

## DO NOT
- Do not tell a caller their specific equipment is or is not covered from these
  notes. Coverage depends on the install date and the registration; look the
  account up.
